FAQ

Questions

Windows says "Windows protected your PC." Is that normal?

Yes. Windows shows this for new apps that aren't signed with a paid certificate. Click More info, then Run anyway. You only have to do it once. The full source code is public if you want to check it.

What do 3L, 4L and 4C mean?

The number is the length and the letter is the type: L = letters only, N = numbers only, C = mixed characters. So zek is a 3L and k1k1 is a 4C. Anything with 3 characters, or 4 letters, is tagged OG.

Are there really open 3Ls left?

On big platforms they're rare, and that's why you scan. Results vary a lot by platform: some still have open 4Ls and plenty of dictionary words, and Minecraft drops free up OGs all the time.

If it says "open," is the name definitely claimable?

Usually, but not always. Platforms sometimes reserve names, block words, or hold names from banned accounts. Instagram and TikTok are the least predictable. Anything the app can't confirm shows as "Unknown," never as open.

Why does it open in my browser?

Your browser is just the window. Everything runs on your own PC, at an address only your computer can reach. The only traffic is the name lookups to each platform.

How do I close it?

Click Quit app at the bottom left. If you just close the tab, the app shuts itself down after a few minutes.
